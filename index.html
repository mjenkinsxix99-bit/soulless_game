version 1.665

Soulless Changelog
v 1.665

Spells

  • Mana Hungry now actually regenerates Mana: 0.5% of your max Mana per second for its duration (1.5%/s when boosted by Power of 3). Previously it only scaled the tiny per-kill Mana trickle, so it appeared to do nothing.
  • Power of 3 no longer runs on a timer — it stays armed until your next spell consumes it. It still clears on Ascension and Reincarnation, and can't be re-armed (or waste Mana) while already armed. Its tooltip now reads "Armed" instead of a countdown.

Interface

  • The tooltips for the first two spells no longer open off the left edge of the screen.
  • Minion XP bars and level badges now update live while the Minion Compendium is open, instead of freezing until it's reopened.
v 1.66

Minions

  • Minion max (Elite) level is now 666, down from 777. The XP curve was retuned — minions become Elite sooner.
  • The DPS minion bonus now applies to DPS only. Strike/click damage stays with the strike-damage minion; its card now reads "bonus to DPS."
  • The minion upgrade-cost reduction now updates upgrade prices the moment you capture or release a minion, and the discount shows even on upgrades you can't yet afford.
  • The minion spell-duration bonus now includes Flurry of Strikes

Sacrifice — Bonus Fixes

  • Fixed the four outer clusters (Power of 3, Mana Hungry, Invoke Power, Twin Strikes): their node bonuses were shifted one cluster off their labels, so nodes didn't do what they said.
  • Tattered Souls from upgrades is now a flat 666:1 (one per 666 upgrades purchased), no longer affected by minion or Sacrifice Grid bonuses.
  • The minion Tattered Souls bonus and the Sacrifice Grid's Tattered Souls nodes now boost Tattered Souls gained from Elite bounties instead as was originally intended (descriptions updated to match).
  • Form, ascension-path, and honor bonuses still apply to all Tattered Souls gained.

Honors — Pentagrams

  • Completing a full pentagram (a spell node plus its five surrounding nodes) earns an honor and grants a ×1.11 multiplier to Souls gained while that pentagram stays complete — its own multiplier, separate from every other soul bonus. All nine at once roughly doubles your souls. The Sacrifice Grid resets on reincarnation, so this is re-earned each life.
  • First Seal — complete your first pentagram. Adept of the Circle — complete five.
  • The Seal of the 9 — complete all nine pentagrams at once. Every life then begins with Flurry's five outer nodes already sealed.
  • Star of Sacrifice — own every node in the Sacrifice Grid at once. Every life then begins with 5 Tattered Souls banked.
v 1.65

Augments

  • Brimstone gains a 5th augment slot at 175 owned: ×1.333 to all upgrade DPS (applied to each upgrade individually).

Upgrades — Late-Game Rebalance

  • Chaos Tide through Spirit Siphon have been rebalanced for a smooth power curve. Buying Chaos Tide no longer triggers a sudden DPS explosion that vaults you hundreds of floors.
  • Your existing progress is preserved: upgrades you already own keep the exact DPS they had — nothing is lost or suddenly changed on login. Only new purchases follow the rebalanced curve.

Spells — Invoke Power

  • Invoke Power's bonus now works properly, showing the change in DPS.
  • Retuned: now caps at 5 stacks and grants +15.54% per stack (+77.7% at max), replacing the old +6.66% over 10 stacks.
  • Added a stack counter to the top-left of the spell button.
  • Invoke Power stacks now properly survive Ascensions, but not Reincarnations.

Artwork

  • Added icons for the late-game upgrades, previously blank.

UI

  • Larger upgrade icons.
  • Larger spell-button and Sacrifice-grid node images.
  • Larger number-key badges on the spell buttons.
  • Spells properly cast on numerical keyboard inputs.
v 1.64

Floor & Realm Progression

  • Removed the delay between floors when auto-progressing.
  • Elite → next-level and elite respawn delays reduced.
  • Removed the "Realm complete" banner and its 2.5s pause.
  • Fixed auto-progress hanging on the boundary elite when re-crossing an already-completed realm barrier.

Striker Honors

  • Click achievements now count all strike sources: manual clicks, Flurry spell strikes, and each minion clicker.
  • New strike tiers added: 10, 100, 666, and 5,000.
  • Strike achievements now grant a starting-souls bonus, applied on every new life.

Spells

  • Invoke Power button no longer keeps flashing after use.
  • Fixed spell tooltips blinking on the panel's refresh.

UI

  • Tabs now open at the top of their content; the Upgrades tab remembers your scroll position when you leave and return.
v 1.63

Performance — Minion Clickers

  • Fixed heavy lag while minion clickers were active.

Honors

  • Fixed achievements not being rewarded.

Minions

  • Multiple minions can now be selected for capture, up to MP available.
  • Multiple minions can now be released with the Release Minion button, refunding MP.
  • Fixed minion portrait joining combat animation when not included.
  • 20 new achievements
  • Fixed minion card brightness

Settings

  • Settings menu added toggles for animation, one for soul drop, one for attack flash.

Augments

  • Reworked to compounding ×2; 4 augments per upgrade at new unlock levels to keep upgrades relevant when affordable.
  • Redistributed the special effects
  • Recolored augment slots (purple / red / green / gold / white)

Spells

  • Owned spell buttons and purchased spell nodes now have a white background; spell node name labels removed
  • Spells dimmed when not enough mana
  • Full mana bar after ascension

Special thanks to u/Forward_Plantain2026, u/SummitSummit, and to Tiffany W.

v 1.62

Hotfix — Save / Load

  • Fixed import bug where loading a save showed progress at 0.
  • Export file now properly holds time and progress.
  • Import file now reflects time saved and progress, awarding up to 6 hours of offline time at 50% efficiency.
v 1.60

Mana

  • Audited mana gain and cap — confirmed nothing was blocking either.
  • Brightened mana bar text so it's legible.
  • Converted mana bar from vertical to horizontal layout.

Reincarnate

  • Non-elite minions now refund their exact MP cost on reincarnate.
  • Reincarnate modal now shows how much MP will be refunded before confirming.
  • Added reincarnate info line detailing what is kept, lost, and refunded.

MP Cost System — Realm Tier Pricing

  • Replaced old scaling cost curve with flat per-realm pricing: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, 55.
  • Releasing a minion refunds the exact tier cost for that minion's realm.

Pet Minion — 2x XP

  • Any minion selected as your Pet now earns 2x XP.
  • Added a 3-second hover tooltip on the Pet button explaining the bonus.

Minion Cards

  • Minion sprites are dimmed when a minion has been captured before but is not currently held this reincarnation.

Tattered Shop

  • Updated shop header text: "666 Upgrades = 1 TS. Every TS gives +6.66% increase to DPS. Bounties on Elites on floor 150+ = Bonus TS!"
  • Spell purchase nodes now display pixel art sprites instead of text glyphs.

Spell Panel

  • All 9 spells are always visible; unpurchased spells show a locked padlock with a tooltip directing players to the Sacrifice tab.
  • Spell panel and mana bar repositioned to anchor to the left edge of the game, spanning into the battlefield area.
  • Each spell button displays its pixel art sprite at full opacity regardless of lock state.
  • Keyboard shortcuts 1–9 cast the corresponding spell.
  • Key number shown in the corner of each spell button and in the tooltip.

Settings

  • Settings cog moved into the buy quantity selector column, below the Buy All Augs button.

Loading Screen

  • Added a yellow animated progress bar on every page load while the game initializes.

Offline Progress

  • New full-screen catchup screen: time away label, purple XP-style fill bar (5s under 3h, 8s over 3h), live souls counter, and efficiency note.
  • Offline progress now also triggers when returning to a hidden tab — not only on full page reloads.
  • Replaced rough DPS estimate with a real kill-cycle simulation using actual enemy HP, your DPS, and average spawn delay for accurate offline rewards.
  • Maximum 6 hours of offline time awarded at 50% efficiency.
